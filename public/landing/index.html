The problem

Running a gym without software is painful

Most gym owners still use spreadsheets, notebooks, and WhatsApp groups. It costs them money every single day.

💸

Members skip payments

Without automated tracking, expired members keep using the gym. You lose thousands every month without even knowing.

📋

Everything is manual

Attendance in a register. Billing in Excel. Trainer schedules on WhatsApp. One mistake and your whole day is wasted.

📊

Zero visibility into your business

You cannot see which plan sells best, when members drop off, or how much revenue you actually made this month.

The solution

Know exactly who walks in — instantly

Every member gets a unique QR code. Your receptionist scans it. The system instantly checks their subscription and logs attendance. No guessing. No arguments.

  • Automatically block expired members
  • See peak hours and attendance trends
  • Prevent QR sharing with daily check-in limits
